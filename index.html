# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 2026** Reseller Crosslister is a personal tool used to manage and cross-list items between online marketplaces. ## Information the Application Processes Reseller Crosslister may process information associated with marketplace listings, including: * Listing titles * Descriptions * Prices * Brands * Sizes * Colors * Conditions * Categories * Listing photos * Marketplace listing identifiers The application may also process authentication information and authorization tokens provided by marketplace platforms when necessary to perform actions that the user has authorized. ## How Information Is Used Information is used solely to manage and cross-list the user's marketplace inventory and to perform actions requested by the user. The application does not sell or rent personal information to third parties. ## Data Storage Listing information and application credentials may be stored locally on the user's computer for the operation of the application. The application does not intentionally collect or store information unrelated to its marketplace-management functions. ## Third-Party Services Reseller Crosslister may interact with third-party marketplace services, including eBay, when authorized by the user. Information exchanged with those services is subject to their respective privacy policies and terms. ## Data Security The user is responsible for maintaining the security of their computer, application credentials, and authentication information used with Reseller Crosslister. ## Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated as the application develops or its functionality changes. ## Contact For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the associated marketplace or application account.
